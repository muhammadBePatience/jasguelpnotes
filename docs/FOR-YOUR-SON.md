# Using the site

## Put it on your phone (do this once)

This is the important bit — once installed, **it works with no signal at all**.
Notes, flashcards, everything. Tunnels, basements, planes, a bus with bad
reception: it all still opens.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the site in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow)
3. Scroll down → **Add to Home Screen** → Add

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the site in Chrome
2. Tap the ⋮ menu
3. **Install app** (or *Add to Home Screen*)

It now behaves like a normal app: its own icon, no browser bars, opens
instantly. Let it sit on wifi for a few seconds the first time so it can
download everything.

When you're offline you'll see a small *"Offline — showing your saved copy"*
note in the menu. That's it working as intended. Next time you're online it
quietly pulls down anything new.

## Switching the theme

Bottom of the menu — **Switch to light** / **Switch to dark**. It remembers,
so you only pick once. Dark for the bus at night, light in daylight.

On a phone the ◐ button in the top-right does the same thing.

## Getting around

- **☰** (top-left, on a phone) opens the course menu
- **Search** on the home page searches every lecture, note and flashcard at
  once — usually faster than clicking through
- On a lecture: **swipe left/right** to move between lectures, or **← / →**
  arrow keys on a laptop
- **Mark as reviewed** when you're done with a lecture — the progress bars
  everywhere update, and the home page always offers you the next unreviewed one

## Quiz me

The **⚡ Quiz me** button pulls flashcards from every course, one at a time.

- Tap the card, or press **Space**, to reveal the answer
- Then **Got it** or **Not yet** — or **→** and **←** on a keyboard
- Anything you get wrong comes back roughly six times as often as something
  you've already mastered, and new cards mix in between

There's no end to a session — it keeps going until you stop, which is what you
want when you don't know how long the ride is.

*Reset stats* clears which cards you've mastered. It does **not** touch which
lectures you've marked reviewed.

## One thing to know

Progress is saved **per device**. Your phone and your laptop each keep their
own record of what you've reviewed and which cards you've mastered. There's no
account and nothing is uploaded anywhere — the trade-off is that the two don't
sync.
